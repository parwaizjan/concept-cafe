# Client checklist — what we need from a new café

This is a **client-facing** document. It is written for a café owner, not for a
developer. Use it during the pitch and to collect everything needed to build and
launch their site. It is safe to copy the questions or the ready-to-send WhatsApp
message at the bottom straight to the owner.

Do not add developer jargon, internal architecture, or credentials to this file.

## What the café gets

- A fast, mobile-friendly website that helps people **find you, see the vibe and
  walk in**.
- Your full menu with prices, laid out clearly.
- Your photos — the space and the food.
- Your location, opening hours, an embedded map and a **one-tap "Get directions"**
  button that opens Google Maps on the customer's phone.
- A WhatsApp **"Message us"** button for group bookings, catering and questions.
- The owner can update **prices, sold-out items and today's specials** themselves,
  for free, from a simple Google Sheet.
- Website hosting costs the café **nothing per month**.
- When live, the site can appear on Google and be shared on Facebook and WhatsApp
  with a proper preview. Google Business Profile setup is included.
- Sinhala and Tamil can be added on request (English is included).
- If you want online ordering, delivery-app links, table/group booking, loyalty or
  an events calendar, these can be added — ask us for a price.

## 1. Business basics

- [ ] Café / brand name (exactly as it should appear).
- [ ] A short tagline or one-line description.
- [ ] A short "our story" paragraph (a few sentences; we can polish it).
- [ ] Logo file, if you have one (PNG or SVG on a plain or transparent background).
- [ ] Brand colours — or tell us to choose for them.
- [ ] Any font or style they must match (optional).
- [ ] Photos for the top of the page and the gallery.

## 2. Contact details

- [ ] **WhatsApp number that should receive messages** (with country code).
- [ ] Phone number shown on the site.
- [ ] Email (optional — for display only; enquiries go to WhatsApp).
- [ ] Facebook / Instagram / TikTok links (optional).
- [ ] Who replies to messages, and roughly when they are available.

## 3. Location and opening hours

- [ ] Full address as it should be written (including the area, e.g. Colombo 05).
- [ ] Google Maps location — a shared pin/link is perfect (we need the exact spot
      so "Get directions" lands on the door).
- [ ] Opening hours for each day, including closed days and any split hours.
- [ ] Special or holiday hours, if any (optional).
- [ ] Parking / landmark directions, if useful (optional).

## 4. Menu and prices

For every item, we need:

- [ ] Category (e.g. Coffee, Not Coffee, Brunch & Plates, Sweets & Bakes).
- [ ] Item name.
- [ ] Price in LKR.
- [ ] A short one-sentence description (optional but recommended).
- [ ] Whether it is vegetarian (a small marker is shown for veg items).
- [ ] A photo (optional to start — we can launch with placeholders and add photos
      later).
- [ ] Sinhala and/or Tamil name, if they have it (optional; we can arrange
      translation).
- [ ] Which items are your specialities or should be featured.
- [ ] Any items that are often sold out or only available on certain days.

A simple table is enough — the owner can fill it in a note or a spreadsheet:

| Category | Item          | Price (LKR) | Veg | Description                     | Photo? |
| -------- | ------------- | ----------- | --- | ------------------------------- | ------ |
| Coffee   | Flat White    | 850         | yes | House espresso with silky milk  | yes    |
| Brunch   | Avocado Toast | 1450        | yes | Sourdough, smashed avocado, egg | no     |

We will handle item IDs, image sizing and formatting — the owner just supplies the
above.

## 5. What's on

- [ ] Weekly events or regulars (e.g. "Vinyl Sundays", "live music Friday").
- [ ] Seasonal or limited items (e.g. "iced matcha for summer").
- [ ] Any classes, tastings or community nights.

## 6. Photos

- [ ] A wide photo for the top of the page (the space, or a beautiful coffee/serve).
- [ ] Gallery photos: the interior, the coffee, the food, the counter, the street
      front (as many as possible — natural light, no screenshots or watermarks).
- [ ] The owner or team (optional).
- [ ] Any logos or signage.

Tips to share with the owner:

- Send the **original** photos, not forwards/screenshots. WhatsApp compresses
  images — for important photos, send as a **document** or share a Google
  Drive / Google Photos link.
- Natural daylight works best; keep the background tidy.
- It is fine to start with a few photos and add more later.

## 7. Price and availability updates (Google Sheet)

- [ ] A Gmail address the café controls (ideally a dedicated café account, not a
      personal one) so we can set up their menu sheet.
- [ ] The person who will update prices and mark items sold out.
- [ ] We set up the Sheet, show them how to use it, and keep a backup of the
      current prices on our side.

## 8. Reviews

- [ ] 2–4 short customer quotes, with first name or initial (optional).
- [ ] We can use generic placeholders until you have quotes you like.

## 9. Domain and languages

- [ ] Preferred web address. A free option is `<cafe-name>.zuvo.lk`. The owner can
      also use their own domain if they have one (or want to buy one).
- [ ] Languages needed: English is included. Sinhala and/or Tamil can be added.
- [ ] Which language should be the default?

## 10. Sign-off

- [ ] The person who approves the final site.
- [ ] Any existing brand guidelines or marketing material to match.
- [ ] A deadline or event the site should be ready for (optional).

## What we do NOT need

- No payment gateway or online payments.
- No booking/calendar account.
- No bank details or payment credentials.
- No hosting or server accounts — hosting is included and free.
- No technical skills from the owner.
- No professional writing or professional photos to get started.

## What happens next

1. You send us the items above (in any order).
2. We build the site and show you a preview link.
3. You review and tell us what to change.
4. We connect the café's WhatsApp number and Google Sheet.
5. The site goes live on the chosen web address.
6. We show the owner how to update prices, mark items sold out, and flag specials.

